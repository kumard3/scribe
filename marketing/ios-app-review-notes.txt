BOLKIT 1.1, REVIEW NOTES

NEW IN 1.1
- Spoken corrections: saying "delete everything I said" or "start over" while dictating keeps only
  what follows. With an optional on-device AI model, self-corrections ("at 2, actually 3") are
  applied; the result is only accepted when words were removed, never added.
- Models screen: optional on-device AI models (Gemma 4 E2B/E4B, Gemma 3 1B, Qwen 2.5 1.5B),
  one-time downloads from Hugging Face, run locally with llama.cpp.
- Cloud accounts (optional, off by default): the user can enter their own Azure Speech, Azure
  OpenAI or OpenAI-compatible key. Keys are stored in the iOS Keychain. Only then does audio or
  text leave the device, to the user's own account.
- Settings > Your stats: word counts, speaking speed, streak and a count of the user's own cloud
  requests, stored only on the device.

NO ACCOUNT, NO PURCHASES
There is no sign up, login, account, or account deletion flow, because the app has no
accounts and no backend. No in-app purchase or subscription. No demo credentials needed.
Free and open source under the MIT licence.

WHAT IT DOES
Bolkit is a dictation app. You speak, it writes the text on the device. Mainstream
dictation apps stream your voice to a company server and charge monthly. Bolkit runs the
speech models locally, works with the network off, and we operate no server.

HOW TO REACH THE MAIN FEATURES
1. Launch. Onboarding runs once, then the record screen opens.
2. Allow the microphone and speech recognition prompts.
3. Tap the large button, speak, tap again. The transcript appears and can be copied. No
   download is required, the default engine is the phone's built-in one.
4. Models: every entry other than the default is an optional one-time download of an
   offline speech model. Pick one and dictation then runs fully offline.
5. Import: pick an m4a or mp3 and get a transcript.
6. History: past transcripts, stored only on the device, deletable.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Apple Speech framework (SFSpeechRecognizer), the default engine.
- Hugging Face and GitHub Releases: static file downloads only, for the optional offline
  models. No API key, no account, nothing sent up.
- Google ML Kit on-device Translation, bundled. Downloads a language pack once, then
  translates locally.
- Optional, off by default: the user may enter their own Azure Speech, Azure OpenAI or
  OpenAI-compatible key (Models > Cloud accounts). Only then does audio or text leave the
  device, to the provider they chose. No key ships in the app.
No authentication provider, payment processor, analytics, advertising SDK, crash reporting,
or backend operated by us.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Identical behaviour in every region, no geofenced features, no regional pricing.

REGULATED INDUSTRY AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not a regulated industry; a productivity utility with no health, financial, gambling or
medical functionality. All speech models are publicly released open-weight models (Whisper,
MIT; NVIDIA NeMo via k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx, Apache 2.0/CC-BY; Google Gemma, Gemma Terms of
Use). Our source is MIT and public at https://github.com/kumard3/scribe.
